Privacy, terms and support

Little Light is a gentle Bible adventure game for children, with a companion web app for families. This is where we explain, simply and honestly, how we look after you and your children.

Our promise in one breath

  • The game collects no personal information from children or anyone else. Progress stays on your device.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics in the game, and no third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • Chapter 1 is free. One optional one-time purchase, made by a grown-up behind a parent check, opens the rest of Volume 1. No subscriptions, no other in-app purchases, nothing aimed at getting children to spend.
  • No chat, no accounts for children, no social features.
